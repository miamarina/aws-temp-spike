- A vector is fundamentally an ordered list of numbers that can represent magnitude and direction in space.  
- In physics, a vector is visualized as an arrow whose length (magnitude) and direction define it; its position can change without altering the vector.  
- In computer science, the focus is on the vector as a data structure—an ordered collection of components, often used for calculations and algorithmic manipulation.
